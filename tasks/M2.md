# M2 — V0.2 physical atmosphere, Sun, space background and HDR

**Prerequisite:** reliable Earth surface and depth (M1).

## Tasks

1. Establish shared units, planet radii, geocentric solar direction, ray intersection and scene depth convention; verify using high/low orbit and ground.
2. Develop physical-inspired Rayleigh/Mie and approximate multiple scattering with coherent transmittance and aerial perspective; validate against authoritative literature/UE5 reference; do not copy incompatible licenses.
3. Render Sun disk with actual position, Earth occultation, approximate angular size, light intensity and restrained HDR glare/bloom.
4. Add stars/cubemap sky with no camera-translation parallax; plausible Moon and moonlight; transition to bright daylight atmosphere.
5. Provide HDR surface/sky composition, explicit exposure and tone mapping; ensure no Sun, terrain or clouds color-space conflict.
6. Validate orbital sunrise, twilight/day/night, aerial perspective, HDR, A07–A10 and WebGL regression.
7. As an explicit M2.6 A10 supplement, add restrained HDR bloom and subtle radial Sun Rays around the solar source, together with atmospheric glow. Composite bloom in linear HDR before ACES/sRGB output; keep rays tied to the true scene-time Sun direction and camera projection. Preserve physical disk/terrain/Earth occultation and atmospheric attenuation, and verify no glow leakage, whole-frame washout or temporal instability. Capture enabled/baseline comparisons across representative Sun elevations and exposures.

## Non-goals

Volumetric clouds, cinematic lens ghosts/dirt/anamorphic streaks or exaggerated lens flare, detailed star catalogs, complete PBR engine, SSGI and automatic exposure. The narrowly scoped M2.6 HDR bloom and restrained radial Sun Rays are explicitly included; they must not be expanded into a general cinematic lens-effects system. Atmospheric/solar effects must be tied to planet curvature rather than camera-local sky fog.

## Safe parallelism

Read-only mathematical research, sky assets and exposure benchmark fixtures can be parallel. **Shader bindings and shared compositing are single-owner until integrated.**
