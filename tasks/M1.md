## Critical acceptance

**A fixed-color sphere or single static Earth texture is not M1 completion.** Actual provider-driven imagery and terrain tiles must function. Fast camera flight between space and ground must avoid evident jitter, z-fighting, holes and invalid LOD transitions.

Visual acceptance follows `docs/ACCEPTANCE.md`: harmless image differences are allowed and pixel-identical WebGL/WebGPU output is not required. All functional and regression gates still run.
