# M1 — V0.1 real WGS84 globe, imagery and Terrain

**Prerequisite:** M0 WebGL baseline and minimal WebGPU pass.

## Ordered vertical slices
1. Inspect actual Globe/surface tile code, WGS84 and camera/frustum/depth mechanisms at Cesium 1.146.0.
2. Render Earth ellipsoid with correct camera/geocentric transforms, accurate horizon and depth handling from orbit to surface.
3. Reuse official ImageryProvider data/tiling logic; update real tile textures, sample imagery, handle LOD, visibility and resource release.
4. Reuse TerrainProvider data with ellipsoid + quantized mesh support; correctly render tiles and drape imagery across changing LOD; inspect adjacent tile cracks.
5. Verify normal camera interaction, resize, tile churn and memory handling; preserve relevant public Globe/Camera interfaces.
6. Capture A03–A06 from `docs/ACCEPTANCE.md` and rerun WebGL baseline.

## Critical acceptance
**A fixed-color sphere or single static Earth texture is not M1 completion.** Actual provider-driven imagery and terrain tiles must function. Fast camera flight between space and ground must avoid evident jitter, z-fighting, holes and invalid LOD transitions.

## Parallelism
Provider compatibility audit, terrain fixtures and numeric precision tests may run in distinct files/worktrees; **shared Globe drawing pipeline and render submission require one owner.**
