WebGPU Globe

Try the experimental WGS84 Globe renderer directly in your browser. The demos explore imagery, terrain, volumetric clouds and the shadows they cast on the ground.

WebGPU Globe Interactive Earth with Google Maps 2D Satellite imagery and Cesium World Terrain through Cesium ion. Enter your own read-only ion token when prompted. Includes M3-01 curved cloud-shell geometry preview with orbital, high-altitude and near-ground views. Open globe demo → Volumetric clouds Switch between cloud types, watch cloud growth, and explore twilight and morning light shafts from preset camera views. Open cloud showcase → Terrain cloud shadows Follow moving clouds over the Golden Gate Strait. Compare direct and ambient dimming, change the Sun’s angular radius, and follow the shadows from the ground to orbit. Open cloud shadow demo → M5 reference scenes Revisit eight fixed views of the globe, sky, clouds and terrain. Replay the same camera motion frame by frame. Open reference scenes → WebGPU Proof Minimal WGSL triangle and depth-testing renderer. Use this if you want to verify that your browser can initialize WebGPU. Open proof demo →
Requires a browser with WebGPU support and a secure context. Google satellite and Cesium World Terrain require an ion token with access to imagery asset 3830182 and terrain asset 1. The demo uses its configured default token first; you can enter another token on the Globe page. Manually entered tokens are not saved by the demo. Experimental M1: fast ground-descent navigation has a known limitation.
