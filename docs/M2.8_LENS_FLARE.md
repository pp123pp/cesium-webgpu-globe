## Chosen hybrid and alternatives

Pure Bloom would soften the source but would not provide camera optics. A full HDRP-style Bloom pyramid/ghost/streak system would exceed B and add unnecessary targets. Choose original analytic optical layers driven by the existing scene HDR within the visible solar disk, measured on the GPU once per frame. This combines data-driven shape/intensity control with screen-space HDR energy without importing another engine.

A 1x1 rgba16float target stores disk-area HDR energy and visibility. A dense deterministic disk sampling pattern projects the real Sun through the existing camera basis; each sample checks WGS84 and native reversed depth (zero is clear), then reads the existing HDR scene at a texel whose center is also inside the visible solar disk. This is a disk-window scene-energy measurement including its atmospheric background, not a separately isolated Sun-radiance buffer. Out-of-view/behind-camera/covered samples contribute zero. Threshold scene-linear highlights, not exposed LDR pixels. The measured average naturally attenuates partial occultation and is zero when fully hidden. No CPU readback or temporal history in production.

The initial compact shoulder and weak reflection were rejected by the user. The revised treatment uses a bright irregular optical core, gold shoulder, broad peach diffusion, soft wavelength-separated arc and a few overlapping olive/yellow reflections. Reuse two original scalar solar ray masks with radial pedestal subtraction and angular filtering to avoid quantized concentric bands and needle-like spokes. No new texture allocation or external artwork is required. A fixed horizontal film gate keeps optical scale consistent between the reference ultrawide crop and a standard viewport. Suppress offscreen sources rather than guess missing terrain visibility. Keep receiver WGS84/depth clipping to satisfy the requested no-Earth/no-terrain leakage boundary; this is deliberately more conservative than real photographic lens optics.

The optical contribution joins scene-linear color before existing single exposure/ACES/sRGB. Flare is independent of Bloom enable. Default `lensFlare.enabled=false`; `strength=1`, `diffusion=1`, `reflection=1` (artist multipliers). Original final entry point remains the disabled route. Shared renderer owns initialization, fixed 1x1 target, uniforms, binding invalidation on resize and destruction. No new network asset. Pipeline failure uses existing initialization cleanup; device loss uses existing destroy/recreate policy.
