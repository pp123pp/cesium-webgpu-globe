one WGSL snippet define the interface for M2.2, without allocating an unused
render pass or rendering new effects.

## Exact contracts

- Every world distance and CPU/GPU ray parameter is in meters. Directions are
  dimensionless and normalized. WGS84 radii come from `Ellipsoid.WGS84`.
- The default atmosphere is an enclosing sphere, radius
  `ellipsoid.maximumRadius + 100000`. Its height is measured above the maximum
  radius, not geodetic height at every latitude. The flattening discrepancy is
  explicitly documented and never changes real terrain positions.
- `WebGPUPlanetMath.createPlanetParameters(ellipsoid, atmosphereHeight)` supplies
  the radii, inverse radii, boundary sphere and approximation error.
- `computeSunDirectionWC(time, result)` follows native `UniformState`: Simon
  inertial Sun position, native celestial-to-fixed transform/fallback,
  normalize. Direction is Earth center to Sun, independent of camera translation.
- `intersectEllipsoid(ray, ellipsoid, result)` and
  `intersectAtmosphere(ray, planet, result)` return a sorted forward `Interval`
  in meters or `undefined`. Normalize finite nonzero input directions; invalid
  inputs throw. Surface/tangent zero-length contact is distinct from occlusion.
- `clipAtmosphereRay(ray, planet, sceneDistance, result)` clips the atmosphere
  segment by positive Earth obstruction and real scene ray distance. An origin
  inside the solid ellipsoid produces no visible atmosphere segment.
- `WebGPUDepth.computeProjection(frustum, result)` preserves native X/Y and the
  M1 analytic finite reversed-Z coefficients. `reconstructEyeDistance` and
  `reconstructRayDistance` distinguish axial distance from unit-ray distance.
  Clear depth zero means background; it is never treated as the near plane.
- `WebGPUPlanetState(ellipsoid, atmosphereHeight).update(camera, time)` owns
  CPU-double camera/Sun state and a 192-byte, vec4-aligned Float32Array.
  `WebGPUPlanetShader` documents and consumes exactly this layout; its helper
  functions have no bindings or entry points until a consumer supplies them.
- `WebGPUScene._planetState` updates after same-frame camera/event callbacks.
  The existing globe remains visually unchanged. Scene depth becomes readable
  by a later, separate GPU pass, never sampled while attached for writing.

## Review focus

1. Exact polar/equatorial surface starts, inward/outward/tangent rays and zero
   direction must not produce half exits, false occlusion or NaN.
2. High orbit and ground precision must preserve CPU cancellation and never add
   high+low ECEF values in float32 before subtracting large positions.
3. Off-center and rotated cameras need noncentral ray-distance reconstruction;
   ray length must not be confused with axial depth or geodetic height.
4. Time and camera changes made in existing callbacks must affect the same
   frame; the Sun must not acquire camera-translation parallax.
5. Depth readability/resize/teardown must retain M0 proof behavior and M1
   greater/equal ordering with no new resource leaks.
