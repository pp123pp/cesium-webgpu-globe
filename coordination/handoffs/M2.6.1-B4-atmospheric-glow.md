## Diagnosis and remaining issues

The integration owner and independent reviewer opened original PNGs. The corrected exposure frames retain an assessable disk, atmospheric limb and Earth. Saturated horizon colors, prominent radial artwork and slight silhouette stair steps remain visual-review concerns.

Yellow twilight and weak disk contrast persist with artwork and Bloom disabled, and that control is byte-identical to the accepted baseline. Physical wavelength-dependent transport/display balance is a major contribution; Bloom measurably changes sky color/brightness and reduces disk contrast further. Authored artwork adds a smaller local veil. The component comparison is bounded to the sampled solar pixel and stated image regions. No B4-specific new defect justified runtime retuning, changing physical coefficients or lowering exposure.

The corrected −8° camera includes sky, horizon and a geographic night-side surface, but the existing unlit constant-color surface remains too bright on both versions. It does not pass realistic dark-terrain lighting. The smooth controlled terrain occluder also limits scenic realism. These remain separately scoped atmosphere/HDR and surface-lighting follow-ups, not acceptance waivers.
