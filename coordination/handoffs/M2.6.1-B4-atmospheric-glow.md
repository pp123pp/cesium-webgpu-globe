## Implementation

The Sun shader receives physical view-path transmittance from the atmosphere composite. A bounded attenuation factor applies only to authored Rays, Halo and Corona when the camera-to-scene path is optically thick. The direct photosphere, physical Sun parameters, direction, disk radius, Earth/terrain depth occlusion and Rayleigh/Mie radiance remain unchanged. The no-atmosphere path supplies unit transmittance.

No additional pass, texture, buffer or WebGL behavior was introduced. HDR/Bloom/ACES/sRGB ordering is unchanged.
