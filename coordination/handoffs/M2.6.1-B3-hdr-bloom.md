## Implemented change

Only `WebGPUSolarBloomShader.js` changes rendering behavior. The soft knee at threshold 1 with half-width 0.5 runs on every source texel before the 4×4 downsample, preserving small bright contributors and chroma. A normalized 5×5 binomial footprint uses nine bilinear samples in the existing single blur pass, replacing five disconnected diagonal samples. Renderer edits only update the blur labels.

Bloom strength remains 0.35. No exposure, physical solar direction/radius/irradiance, B1/B2 source/artwork, atmosphere transmission, masking, WebGL or tone-mapping order changes. No additional runtime textures, passes or multi-scale chain were necessary.
