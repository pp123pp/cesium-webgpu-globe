## Model decision

Direct nested ray marching avoids LUTs but repeats expensive solar integrals at
every pixel. A complete Bruneton high-dimensional precompute is broader than
this task. Choose small transmittance and reduced Hillaire isotropic multiple
scattering tables plus direct view integration. Independently written equations;
no third-party source copied. Detailed references/units belong in WEBGPU_M2.2.

Density uses radial height above the actual WGS84 ellipsoid, zero at all surface
latitudes. Lookup curvature uses the equatorial reference radius; true ellipsoid
solar occlusion and actual scene depth remain authoritative. The outer sphere is
the M2.1 boundary, and medium density ends at its calibrated 100 km height.
The reduced multiple-scattering closure has no ground bounce and caps feedback
at 0.95; it is an approximation, not the full Hillaire solver.

Model exports `createAtmosphereParameters`, `validateAtmosphereParameters`,
`atmosphereLutKey`, `generateAtmosphereLuts`; the last returns RGBA Float32Array
tables (256x64 transmittance, 32x32 multiple scattering). Renderer uploads
RGBA16Float. `scene.atmosphere` holds mutable settings; `show` controls the effect.
`skyAtmosphere:false` remains a supported constructor disable. `scene._atmosphere`
owns only atmosphere buffers/textures/pipeline; Context owns frame attachments.

WGSL bindings, owned only by integration: 0 planet (192 bytes), 1 parameters
(64 bytes), 2 transmittance LUT, 3 multiple-scattering LUT, 4 filtering sampler,
5 completed scene color, 6 completed scene depth. `atmosphereIntegrate` returns
linear radiance and RGB transmittance. Clear depth produces no surface endpoint.

2. Sun-direction phase sign and direct-light shadowing at true WGS84 dawn/night.

   samples clustered near minimum altitude rather than uniform distance.
