## 2. Executive findings

The current implementation is a bounded visual-effects layer integrated with the physical Sun, atmosphere and depth path. It is not a physically simulated solar surface or corona.

Confirmed facts:

1. The solar disk is analytic angular coverage, not an image texture. Its radius comes from the angular-radius uniform and is antialiased using the pixel angular footprint.
2. Rays, two halos, corona and noise are authored 512² GPU textures. Three ray masks are sampled at the same UV and linearly weighted; the current shader applies no independent rotation, UV warp or per-layer scale offset.
3. Corona uses one fine mask plus one noise modulation texture. There are no separate broad/mid/fine corona frequency bands in the current shader.
4. Eight pre-rasterized payloads are fetched and uploaded once per renderer initialization. SVG is not uploaded directly to WebGPU.
5. The eight payloads total 2,883,584 bytes (2.75 MiB) of logical payload. Seven masks are R8Unorm scalar coverage; the tint ramp is RGBA8Unorm-sRGB.
6. Solar artwork is composed in a scene-linear RGBA16Float target. Bloom extracts highlights before exposure/tone mapping, uses quarter-resolution intermediates and one diagonal blur pass, then applies exposure, fitted ACES and one sRGB encode.
7. Solar fragments are rejected by completed scene depth and WGS84 ray/ellipsoid visibility. Bloom is re-masked against receiver depth and Earth geometry after blur.
8. M2.6 closeout contains actual WebGPU captures and checks for terrain occlusion, exposure, effect isolation, 120-frame progression, resource lifecycle and GPU timestamps. It explicitly retains stylized golden rays and high-exposure low-Sun contrast as limitations.

No confirmed P0 physical-correctness defect was found by static review. Main visual gaps are P1: regular/decorative ray appearance, limited corona frequency structure, and the analytic uniform disk's lack of authored disk surface/highlight variation. Sampling stability at small projected angular footprints is a P2 risk, not a confirmed defect.

## 3. Code trace

### Solar disk

- packages/engine/Source/Scene/WebGPUSun.js: angularRadius is 0.00465 rad (approximately 0.2664° semidiameter); displayScale converts normalized irradiance to scene-linear disk radiance. Defaults include glowStrength 0.08 and glowRadius 0.05 rad.
- packages/engine/Source/Scene/WebGPUSunShader.js: sunDiskCoverage uses chord distance and pixel-width smoothstep. The disk is uniform in this shader; the authored gradient tints surrounding effects, not the disk interior.
- sunSourceVisibility uses 16 disk samples, camera projection and completed depth. Per-pixel disk/effect fragments also reject non-clear depth and Earth intersection.

### Rays, halo and corona

- WebGPUSunShader.js: ray weights are 0.48 / 0.30 / 0.22 for primary/fine/broad masks. All masks share one UV mapping. Halo weights are 0.70 soft and 0.25 wide. Corona uses one corona-fine mask modulated by radial-noise.
- atmosphere.sun.w bounds the effect UV domain; the shader samples explicit LOD 0. No mip chain or derivative-based LOD is selected.
- Multiple assets do not by themselves prove visually independent scales or non-repeating composition.

### Atmosphere and HDR

- packages/engine/Source/Scene/WebGPUAtmosphereShader.js: atmosphereIntegrate provides physical-inspired transport. atmosphereLinearFragment composes background, stars, solar artwork, Moon and atmospheric transport into scene-linear output; sunOnlyLinearFragment preserves the atmosphere-disabled path.
- packages/engine/Source/Scene/WebGPUSolarBloomShader.js: extraction threshold is linear brightness 1.0; source texels are thresholded before 4× downsampling; one diagonal Kawase-like blur is performed; final composition masks blurred glow against depth and Earth before ACES/sRGB.
- packages/engine/Source/Scene/WebGPUAtmosphereRenderer.js: owns RGBA16Float linear and quarter-resolution Bloom A/B targets, reuses them between frames and recreates them on resize.
- packages/engine/Source/Scene/WebGPUScene.js: terrain surface is rendered/prepared before WebGPUGlobeRenderer.render; the renderer's composite callback executes atmosphere/Sun linear composite → Bloom extraction → Bloom blur → final Bloom composition + ACES/sRGB.

## 4. Color, alpha and formats

- Seven R8 payloads store SVG alpha coverage in R and are sampled as linear scalar data. The color ramp is rgba8unorm-srgb and its RGB is decoded to linear by GPU sampling; its alpha is fully opaque.
- The solar shader uses sampled masks as scalar radiance weights. There is no conventional sprite alpha-blend operation, so premultiplied-vs-straight-alpha blend-state ambiguity does not apply to these masks.
- SVG authoring sources are rasterized through Playwright Chromium Canvas from 1024² to 512². Runtime consumes raw R8/RGBA8 payloads, not SVG.
- The sampler uses linear min/mag filtering and clamp-to-edge. No mipmaps are generated. This is confirmed; visible aliasing/flicker requires GPU capture to establish.
- Keep scalar masks linear and authored tint sRGB. Any semantic change must update the manifest, builder, upload format and shader together.

## 5. Pipeline and performance

- Linear composite target: rgba16float. Bloom A/B: rgba16float at ceil(viewport/4) dimensions.
- Solar payload: 2,883,584 bytes. At 1920×1080 the linear + two Bloom target payloads account for 18,662,400 logical bytes. This is not resident VRAM.
- Bloom enabled: linear composite, extract/downsample, blur, final output = four fullscreen render passes in the composite callback. Bloom disabled omits extract and blur but retains linear composite and final output.
- M2.6 hardware timestamp evidence (Intel Xe-LPG, Chromium 156, 1920×1080, 30 alternating samples after warmup): all enabled mean 16.80 ms, p95 20.45 ms; authored effects enabled/Bloom disabled mean 16.53 ms, p95 20.51 ms; mean Bloom increment about 0.27 ms. Historical observation only, not a new measurement or performance guarantee.
- Solar shader samples eight texture values per candidate effect pixel (three ray masks, two halo masks, corona, noise, tint ramp) and evaluates a 16-sample visibility estimate. Cost is spatially bounded by effect angular radius; re-profile if B changes it.
