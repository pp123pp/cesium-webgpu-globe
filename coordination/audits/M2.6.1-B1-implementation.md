## B01 — Solar Disc

Implemented in `packages/engine/Source/Scene/WebGPUSunShader.js` only.

- Added a normalized limb-darkening profile with coefficient 0.60.
- The projected-disk integral is normalized by `(1 - u/3)`; this keeps the integrated disk luminance at its prior calibration.
- Added a restrained warm-white edge tint. Its RGB coefficients are normalized to unit Rec.709 luminance, so the tint changes edge chromaticity rather than total photometric luminance.
- Kept the analytic disk coverage and its pixel-width anti-aliasing.
- Kept `angularRadius = 0.00465 rad`, `displayScale`, scene-time Sun direction, Earth/terrain occlusion and depth behavior unchanged.
- No change was made to `WebGPUSun.js` or the HDR → ACES → sRGB pipeline.

The B1 browser probe measured a 127.83 px projected disk radius at 1920×1080 / 4° FOV. Linear radiance was 6.23 near the center and 4.21 red / 4.06 blue at 90% radius; coverage remained 1.0 there and was 0.0 at 120% radius.

## B02 — Solar Rays

Reused the same three source textures and regenerated their existing GPU payloads. No new texture was added.

- Replaced regular spoke placement with deterministic, irregular angular distributions and varied line lengths, widths, opacity and slight curvature.
- Authored ray counts: Primary 25, Fine 39, Broad 13 (77 total versus 184 in the original regular patterns: 48 / 112 / 24).
- Added fixed per-layer UV transforms: Primary rotation 0.11 rad / scale 0.96; Fine -0.19 rad / 1.07; Broad 0.31 rad / 0.86.
- Updated layer weights to 0.45 / 0.15 / 0.40.
- Reduced the ray contribution multiplier to 2.5 and softened the outer fade.
- No frame-time randomization, camera-dependent offset or fixed screen-space center was introduced.
